Faster downloads with an NCBI API key
A free key from your NCBI account settings raises the limit from 3 to 10 requests per second. It stays in this browser and is only sent to NCBI.

    Data comes directly from PubMed through NCBI's E-utilities. Article types are PubMed's own labels, so very recent papers may not be labelled yet.

    PubMed's coverage before 1966 is thin and many older records have no abstract, so early years undercount what was actually published.
