      How expectation works. If a mechanism appears in R papers, a comparison in C papers, and the field as a whole has N papers, then R × C ÷ N studies would mention both by chance. A pair with 1 study where 6 are expected is a much stronger gap signal than a pair with 1 study where 0.3 are expected.

      Read before you trust a gap. Counts come from words in titles and abstracts. A paper that mentions both species has not necessarily compared them, and a comparison buried in a figure may not be mentioned at all. Download the abstracts for a promising cell and check them. Preprints on bioRxiv are not in PubMed.
